The work

CricketPulse AI

Personal project · flagship

An IPL analytics and news-sentiment system, framed as a data-driven franchise management proposal to BCCI. It analyses 1,095 matches, 260,920 deliveries and 99 live headlines.

Python · pandas · NLP (TextBlob) · NewsAPI · Power BI · BRD

Read more +Show less −
Problem

IPL decisions lean on gut feel and box scores; media narrative and sentiment rarely make it into the picture.

Approach

Multi-season match and ball-by-ball data plus NLP sentiment on live IPL 2026 headlines (NewsAPI + TextBlob).

Delivered

4-page dashboard and a 14-section BRD with stakeholder map, As-Is/To-Be and gap analysis, risk register and 6 prioritised recommendations.

Key findings: toss outcome had close to zero impact on results; Gujarat Titans combined a high win rate with low media coverage; IPL coverage was 89.9% positive, making it a brand-safe property for sponsors; a modelled CSK-ban scenario showed a measurable shift in sentiment.
